Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

In short: Native Desktop Search runs entirely on your computer. Files and data it reads from Google Drive, OneDrive, Notion, or your local folders are used only to build a search index stored on your own device. We do not operate servers that receive your data, and we never sell, share, or transfer it to anyone.

1. Who we are

Native Desktop Search ("the app", "we") is an open-source desktop application for searching your local and cloud files. Source code: github.com/aj757/native-desktop-search. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

2. Data the app accesses

The app only accesses a source after you add or connect it yourself.

Source Data accessed Permission requested
Google Drive File names, file types, modification dates, parent folder IDs, and the text content of your files; your Google account ID and email address (to identify the connected account) https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly (read-only)
Microsoft OneDrive File names, metadata, and content; your account name and ID Microsoft Graph read-only file access
Notion Titles and text of the pages you choose to share with the app Notion read access to selected pages
Local folders and Obsidian vaults File names, paths, and text content of files in folders you select Operating system file access

The app never creates, modifies, moves, shares, or deletes files in your Google Drive or any other source. All cloud access is read-only.

3. How we use your data

Data accessed from these sources is used for one purpose only:

  • to build and update a search index on your computer so that you can search your own files, see text snippets in results, and open the folder containing a result.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

4. Google user data

Native Desktop Search's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, for data obtained through Google APIs:

  • It is used only to provide the search feature described above, which is visible to you in the app.
  • It is not transferred to anyone, except as needed to provide that feature, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with your consent.
  • It is not used or transferred for serving ads, including retargeting or personalised ads.
  • It is not used to determine credit-worthiness or for lending purposes, and it is not sold to data brokers or information resellers.
  • No human reads it. The developers have no access to your Google data, because it never leaves your device.
  • It is not used to develop, improve, or train generalised or non-personalised AI or machine learning models. Any semantic search runs a model locally on your device.

5. Where your data is stored

  • Search index and file text: stored in the app's data folder on your computer (a local database and index files). It is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Sign-in tokens: OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in your operating system's secure credential store (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain, or the Linux Secret Service). Your passwords are never seen or stored by the app; you sign in directly on Google's, Microsoft's, or Notion's own pages.
  • Logs: diagnostic logs are kept locally and never sent to us.

The app communicates only with the services you connect (Google, Microsoft, Notion) and with GitHub to check for app updates. Update checks do not include any of your files or account data.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to any third party. We do not run any server that collects it.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Data is kept on your device only for as long as the source stays connected in the app.
  • Remove a source in the app (Sources → Remove) to delete all indexed data for that source from your device.
  • Revoke access to Google Drive at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. This immediately invalidates the stored tokens.
  • Uninstall the app and delete its data folder to remove everything it stored. Stored sign-in tokens can also be deleted from your operating system's credential manager.

8. Security

All communication with Google, Microsoft, and Notion uses HTTPS. Sign-in uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, and tokens are kept in the operating system's encrypted credential store.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
